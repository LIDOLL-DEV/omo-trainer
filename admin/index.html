How your estimate works
Drink totals cover the time since your previous check-in. The model approximates their timing and tests 30-, 60- and 120-minute response half-lives. These are diary-model assumptions, not measurements of absorption or bladder filling. Missing records, sleep, activity and medicines can change the pattern. The model learns only from this profile's records, works offline, and recalculates after edits or sync.

              
This estimates recorded wettings, not when your body needs to go. Use your diaper whenever you need; don't wait for the estimate or change your drinking to match it.

            

      

      

        
User management

        
Manage Little Log access for accounts that have signed in. Disabling access revokes Little Log sessions and blocks new ones. LiD0llID passwords and access to other apps are managed separately. At least one enabled administrator must remain.

        
        

      

      

        
Import & export

        

          
Export selected participants
Exports include every current record and the complete potty chart for the selected user or everyone. Analysis date filters do not limit these backups. CSV includes readable record fields and JSON cells for full chart rows and dated stars.

          
Import records and charts
Choose a Little Log CSV or JSON file (up to 16 MiB). Files without participant IDs require an individual user selection. Existing user IDs are preserved; imports never create login accounts, change roles or delete missing records.

            
            
            
Identical records are skipped. Replacing a chart replaces the complete chart. Old CSV exports without IDs receive repeatable IDs; identical rows in those files collapse into one record.
