How your estimate works

Drink totals cover the time since your previous check-in. The model approximates their timing and tests 30-, 60- and 120-minute response half-lives. These are diary-model assumptions, not measurements of absorption or bladder filling. Missing records, sleep, activity and medicines can change the pattern. The model learns only from this profile's records, works offline, and recalculates after edits or sync.

This estimates recorded wettings, not when your body needs to go. Use your diaper whenever you need; don't wait for the estimate or change your drinking to match it.

      CHRYSALIS // ACTIVE PROTOCOL

      Daily probability

      50%

      Each completed day: F + SF + V ≥ SI + I decreases the chance by 5 percentage points. F + SF + V < SI + I increases it by 5. A day with no F, SF, V, SI or I events recorded also increases it by 5. Bedwetting and Used the potty are recorded separately and do not enter this comparison. Bounds: 20–80%.
